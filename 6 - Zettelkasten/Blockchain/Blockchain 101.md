# Blockchain 101

### Technical problems that Satoshi needs to Solve

**[[Byzantine Generals Problem]]** - Generals or leaders which often command the 'truth'. Or intermediaries, middle-men and custodians which all act as mutually trusted source between two or more parties. 
Banks, governments, social-media are examples of the 'intermediaries' which we rely on today. Because these are the one managing our transactions of data and money.

**[[Double Spending Problem]]** - Problem of after spending the 'cash', deduction & crediting the amount is in control of these intermediaries. We trust these intermediaries to look after the money and the data because that is how system is designed.

**[[Centralized Structure]]** -  There are problems attache with these intermediaries, although they manage the money pretty well, the attached costs, being *single point of failure* as they stored in single data-centers and other socio-economic problems.
