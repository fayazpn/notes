# Centralized Structure

The four key problems of a centralized world:

- **Single points of failure** - Data-base hacks are one of the most biggest problem in the world. It happens in every industry in all levels, like banking, social-media, Insurance, Utils etc

- **Monopolization & pricing power** -  Payment providers such as **PayPal** has monopoly in their sector, they can decide the % they want to charge from the transaction.

- **Mismanaged economies** - Governments mis-management of the funds leading to inflation has been happening in several developing and developed countries.

- **Economic & social Inequality** - There are parts of population who do not have access to financial structure due to several reasons.
