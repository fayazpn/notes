# Intentional learning

Map: [[Maps/Life and ideas|Life and ideas]]

## Summary

My takeaway from the saved talk: make room for deliberate attention, then turn some consumption into a small piece of useful work.

## Key points

- Choose what to read or watch before opening a recommendation feed.
- Leave quiet time to notice questions worth exploring.
- After a useful source, write an explanation, example, or question in my own words.
- Prefer a sustainable routine and meaningful conversation over guilt-driven targets.
- Review actions I can take next, rather than repeatedly redesigning the whole plan.
