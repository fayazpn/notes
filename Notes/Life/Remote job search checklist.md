# Remote job search checklist

Map: [[Maps/Life and ideas|Life and ideas]]

## Summary

Use evidence of relevant work to explain the value I can bring to a specific team.

## Key points

- Pick roles that match the work I can demonstrate; tailor the portfolio and examples accordingly.
- Research the product and team before investing time in contributions or outreach.
- Where useful, make a focused, tested contribution, integration, or demonstration of their tool.
- Describe the problem, tradeoff, result, and my contribution clearly.
- Check current job requirements and compensation directly; old salary guesses are not a reliable reference.
