# Operators

Map: [[Maps/Go|Go]]

## Summary

Operators combine, compare, or update values. Increment and decrement are statements in Go.

## Key points

- Arithmetic: `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `%`. String `+` concatenates strings.
- Comparison: `==`, `!=`, `<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=` where the operand types support them.
- Boolean logic: `&&`, `||`, `!`; `&&` and `||` short-circuit.
- Bitwise operators include `&`, `|`, `^`, `&^`, `<<`, and `>>`.
- `x++`, `x--`, `x += 2`, and `x -= 2` update variables; increment/decrement are not bitwise operations.
