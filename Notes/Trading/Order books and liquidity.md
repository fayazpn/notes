# Order books and liquidity

Map: [[Maps/Trading|Trading]]

## Summary

An order book organizes resting buy and sell interest by price. The best bid and ask describe only the top of that book.

## Key points

- The bid-ask spread is the gap between the best buy and sell prices.
- Depth is the available quantity across price levels; the displayed best price may cover only a small quantity.
- Adding a resting order supplies liquidity; matching immediately against resting interest consumes it. A limit order can also execute immediately if marketable.
