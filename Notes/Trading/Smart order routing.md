# Smart order routing

Map: [[Maps/Trading|Trading]]

## Summary

A smart order router decides where and in what size to send execution requests across available venues.

## Key points

- Compare executable prices and available quantities with relevant fees and operational constraints.
- A displayed price is not a guaranteed fill; liquidity and conditions may change before execution.
- Keep generic routing concepts separate from claims about a particular implementation's architecture.
