# Centralization tradeoffs

Map: [[Maps/Systems|Systems]]

## Summary

Concentrating control can simplify coordination while creating dependencies on one operator or authority.

## Key points

- Identify the failure domain: a centralized service can still use replicated infrastructure, while a nominally distributed service can share a critical dependency.
- Consider operational complexity, governance, access control, recovery, and trust separately.
- Distribution shifts responsibilities; it does not automatically remove security risks, unequal access, or economic incentives.
